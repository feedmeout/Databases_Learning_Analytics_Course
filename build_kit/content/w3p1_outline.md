6. (2′, LAB1) **Το κατώφλι είναι απόφαση, όχι ιδιότητα του μοντέλου**: P(E→S) with the cut moved: 0,25 / 0,35 / 0,45 / 0,55 → επισημαίνονται 53 / 98 / 142 / 175 · χωρίς λόγο 8 / 25 / 54 / 73 · διαφεύγουν 110 / 82 / 67 / 53. From 0,25 to 0,55: 57 more students found, 65 more alerted for nothing. _[Lab 1 case]_
7. (1′) **Το κατώφλι προκύπτει από το κόστος των δύο σφαλμάτων**: One line of decision theory for a CS cohort: επισήμανση όταν p > C(ψευδώς θετικό) / [C(ψευδώς θετικό) + C(ψευδώς αρνητικό)]. If a missed student costs five times an unnecessary contact, the cut is 1/6, not 0,5. So the real question is what each error costs, and to whom. _[Textbook decision theory (no empirical claim)]_

9. (1′, LAB1) **Ψευδώς αρνητικό: ο φοιτητής που δεν επισημάνθηκε**: 26 people. No support, and nobody audits the students who were not flagged, so the error stays invisible. One-line callback: in the 2019 Turku cohort the model found 35% of those who failed. _[Lab 1 case · Veerasamy et al. (2022), already on Week 2]_
10. (2′, LAB1) **Ψευδώς θετικό: ο φοιτητής που επισημάνθηκε χωρίς λόγο**: 63 people. Four costs. The message itself: even well-made analytics feedback often provokes negative emotion, and here nothing offsets it. The adviser hour taken from someone who needed it: inefficient targeting is one reason dropout programmes show no effect. Alarm fatigue: the list stops being trusted (stated as a risk, not as a finding). Advice that steers the student (the EAB case, slide 21). _[Lim et al. (2021), Assessment & Evaluation in Higher Education 46(3) · Eegdeman et al. (2023)]_
11. (1′) **Το κόστος το ορίζει η παρέμβαση, όχι το μοντέλο**: Three-row table: light action useful to anyone (message with resources) tolerates many false positives → low cut; scarce human action (meeting) → high cut; heavy action (compulsory step, change of track) → almost none. Answers the poll without depending on it. _[Own reasoning (no empirical claim)]_

## Περιορισμένη δυναμικότητα
12. (1′, LAB1) **192 επισημάνσεις, 60 διαθέσιμες συναντήσεις**: Capacity turns the cut into a ranking. The 60 students with the lowest P(E→S): 51 truly at risk (85%), but they are one third of the 155 who will not pass. _[Lab 1 case]_
13. (1′) **Ευστοχία στη δυναμικότητα (precision at k)**: The metric has to match the decision: accuracy and AUC describe the model, precision among the k we can actually see describes the service. Dutch vocational education: «invitation rules» that make the sensitivity/precision trade-off explicit under counselling capacity; inefficient targeting as one reason dropout programmes show no effect. _[Eegdeman, Cornelisz, Meeter & van Klaveren (2023), Education Economics 31(3)]_
14. (1′, SKIP) **Ποιους δεν βλέπει η κατάταξη**: 67 students have no P(E→S) value and cannot be ranked at all; 39 of them will not pass. 39% of working students are in that group against 18% of the rest. The ranking inherits the measurement problem of Lab 1. _[Lab 1 case]_

## Δικαιοσύνη μεταξύ υποομάδων
15. (2′, LAB1) **Ίδιος κανόνας, άνισα σφάλματα**: Among students who PASSED: flagged 90% of working students (26 of 29) against 32% of the others (37 of 114). Among those who did not pass: missed 3% against 21%. The rule over-alerts one group and under-detects the other. The variable «εργάζεται» was never in the rule. _[Lab 1 case]_
16. (1′) **Τρεις ορισμοί της δικαιοσύνης**: Ίσα ποσοστά επισήμανσης (demographic parity) · ίσα ποσοστά σφαλμάτων (equalized odds) · ίση αξιοπιστία της επισήμανσης (predictive parity). Three cards, each with what it promises to the student. _[Definitions as in Chouldechova (2017) · Baker & Hawn (2022), already on Week 1]_
17. (2′) **Οι τρεις ορισμοί δεν ικανοποιούνται ταυτόχρονα**: When the base rates of two groups differ, a score that is equally reliable in both cannot also have equal error rates. Two kinds of unfairness, then: in Lab 1 the base rates were almost equal (55% and 51% did not pass), so the gap came from the measurement and can be repaired; where base rates differ the gap cannot be removed, only chosen, and the choice is written down before deployment. Allowed exception to the 2020+ rule: it is the foundational result. _[Chouldechova (2017), Big Data 5(2), 153-163]_
18. (1′) **Σε πραγματικά δεδομένα: ποιους αδικεί η πρόβλεψη**: Nationally representative US data (ELS:2002), usual features, several ML models: wrongly predicted to fail 19% of Black and 21% of Hispanic students against 12% of White and 6% of Asian students. The leading bias-mitigation techniques were «generally ineffective». _[Gándara, Anahideh, Ison & Picchiarini (2024), AERA Open 10]_
19. (1′, SKIP) **Αρκεί να αφαιρεθεί το προστατευόμενο χαρακτηριστικό;**: No. Large US university, residential and online students, models with and without four protected attributes (gender, under-represented minority, first generation, high financial need): leaving them out improved neither accuracy nor fairness; including them improved fairness marginally. In Lab 1 the rule never saw «εργάζεται» and still treated working students differently. _[Yu, Lee & Kizilcec (2021), L@S '21, 91-100]_

24. (1′) **Τύποι παρεμβάσεων**: Your own typology from the revision deck, in your wording: Αυτοματοποιημένες (emails υπενθύμισης, ειδοποιήσεις, εξατομικευμένα μηνύματα) · Εκπαιδευτικές (συνάντηση με σύμβουλο, επιπλέον υποστήριξη, tutoring) · Εκπαιδευτικού υλικού (προσαρμοσμένο περιεχόμενο, εναλλακτικοί πόροι). _[Your «Μαθησιακή Αναλυτική: Επανάληψη», slide «Συστήματα Έγκαιρης Προειδοποίησης»]_
25. (2′, LAB1) **Δύο δρόμοι, δύο παρεμβάσεις**: The promise of Lab 1 paid: χαμηλή δέσμευση → contact, barriers, goal for the next sheet; χαμηλή δεξιότητα → tutorial lab, content. A deployed European system makes the same split («at risk of failing» against «at risk of dropping out») and tested goal-setting messages on the current activity: 581 of 957 enrolled first-year online learners took part; dropout fell significantly. _[Bañeres, Rodríguez-González, Guerrero-Roldán & Cortadas (2023), IJETHE 20(1), 3]_
26. (2′) **Το ίδιο μήνυμα σε δύο διατυπώσεις**: Left: «Το σύστημα σας κατατάσσει σε υψηλό κίνδυνο αποτυχίας». Right: what was observed, no label, one concrete next step, a named sender. Four rules of wording. Prepares the message the teams write in Lab 2. _[Lim et al. (2021): students valued task focus and the sense that the instructor cares; emotions were often negative even towards good feedback. The rule «one concrete next step» is a design rule, not a finding.]_

## Λειτουργεί;
27. (1′) **Η ακριβής πρόβλεψη δεν αρκεί: πείραμα πεδίου στην Ολλανδία**: Randomised field experiment at a large Dutch university: counsellors received each first-year student's risk. The system predicted accurately; dropout did not fall and performance did not improve. The authors: what is missing is actionable feedback and counselling practice. _[Plak, Cornelisz, Meeter & van Klaveren (2022), Higher Education Quarterly 76(1)]_
28. (2′) **Δέκα χρόνια έγκαιρης προειδοποίησης: Wisconsin DEWS**: More than 200.000 students, AUC 0,80. Effect of the «high risk» label on on-time graduation: +4,8 percentage points, 95% interval from −2 to +11,6: zero cannot be excluded. School-level features target almost as well as individual scores. «The bottleneck is not identification but interventions.» _[Perdomo, Britton, Hardt & Abebe (2023), arXiv:2304.06205]_
29. (2′) **Τι λειτούργησε και τι όχι**: Did not: encouragement e-mails to predicted dropouts in MITx MicroMasters (model found 80% of dropouts, e-mails opened, no effect); a dashboard with predicted pass chance plus weekly e-mail for 556 first-year Java students (more online activity, no effect on the exam). Did: changing the course content that caused the difficulty. Actions that remove the cause outperform messages about the risk. _[Borrella, Caballero-Caballero & Ponce-Cueto (2022), Computers & Education 179 · Hellings & Haelermans (2022), Higher Education 83(1)]_
30. (1′) **Πώς θα γνωρίζετε αν λειτούργησε η δική σας παρέμβαση**: Flagged against not flagged is not a comparison (they differ by construction: the Course Signals error of Week 1). Three designs that are: a lottery among the flagged when capacity is short, comparison just above and below the cut (as in DEWS), phased roll-out. Also a reading rule for the assignment. _[Own synthesis of the designs used by the studies above]_
